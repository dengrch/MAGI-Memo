# MAGI-Memo

MAGI Memory System: A deliberative memory engine for consensus context recall.


> In  *Neon Genesis Evangelion* , the three supercomputers developed by Dr. Naoko Akagi are collectively known as  **MAGI** . MAGI functions as the "brain" of NERV and consists of three subsystems: **MELCHIOR-1**, **BALTHASAR-2**, and **CASPER-3**.
>
> MAGI is designed to resemble human thinking. It can not only simulate the complex and often conflicted decision-making processes of a human being, but also runs on a  **personality-transfer operating system** —a system that transfers a human's patterns of reasoning into a computer. These thought patterns were modeled after Dr. Naoko Akagi herself.
>
> Each of the three MAGI units embodies a different aspect of Dr. Akagi's personality:
>
> * **MELCHIOR-1** contains her personality as a  **scientist** .
> * **BALTHASAR-2** contains her personality as a  **mother** .
> * **CASPER-3** contains her personality as a  **woman** .
>
> Final decisions are reached through deliberation among the three systems, with a structure that effectively allows any one system to veto a decision.
>
> The name **MAGI** comes from the **Three Wise Men (Magi)** of the New Testament. The names  **Melchior** ,  **Balthasar** , and **Casper** are traditionally associated with these three figures. According to Christian tradition, they journeyed from the East to Jerusalem and Bethlehem to honor and bless Jesus upon his birth.
